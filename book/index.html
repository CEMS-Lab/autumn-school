Autumn School — Days 2 and 3#

Choose the materials for your teaching day:

What each practical covers#

Each lab moves through predict → explain → compute → inspect → exercise → worked solution. Short code cells introduce one operation at a time. Expandable setup and implementation details keep the complete calculation available for closer study.

Practical

What you will do

NB1 — Intro to PhAST

Inspect a notched specimen and its loading, run PhAST, and interpret crack propagation, energy curves and saved fields.

NB2 — Inverse Problem using PhAST

Solve a 1D elastic bar with PhAST and recover its Young’s modulus from one force and one observed extension.

NB3 — Hybrid FEM+DL with PhAST

Compare conventional FEM with a frozen graph neural network that replaces the damage subproblem on a three-hole plate.

The optional direct learned-replacement case study connects Lab 3 to a retained Radius-GNO fracture trajectory and reports a matched damage-stage timing comparison.

PhAST ∇

No neural network required.

A differentiable simulation can provide parameter sensitivities on its own. The chain rule was here before the neural network.

.backward()

A pocket chain rule.

Let q = θ2 and L = ½(q − 4)2. At θ = 1, which way does the loss decrease locally?

Follow the derivative

dL/dθ = (q − 4) · 2θ = −6. A sufficiently small positive change in θ decreases the loss. The gradient points uphill; a descent step goes against it.
