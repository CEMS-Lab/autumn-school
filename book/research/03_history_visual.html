Why a fracture solver needs a memory-aware derivative#

A diffuse crack can still contain a sharp mathematical decision. At each material point, the solver asks: is the current crack-driving energy larger than anything this point has experienced before? This short lesson follows that decision through loading, unloading and backpropagation.

1. Remember the largest energy#

\[H_{n+1}=\max(H_n,\psi^+_{n+1}).\]

Here \(H_n\) stores the previous maximum and \(\psi^+\) is the current tensile driving energy. The maximum is continuous, with a kink at equality. Its current-energy partial is zero below the old maximum and one above it. At the tie, a classical partial derivative is generally undefined.

2. Carry the memory backwards#

Sensitivity means how much a quantity changes when an input changes slightly. For any inverse parameter \(\theta\), such as a particle coordinate,

\[ \frac{\mathrm dH_{n+1}}{\mathrm d\theta} =w_H\frac{\mathrm dH_n}{\mathrm d\theta} +w_\psi\frac{\mathrm d\psi^+_{n+1}}{\mathrm d\theta}. \]

For the hard branch rule, \((w_H,w_\psi)=(1,0)\) during unloading and \((0,1)\) at a new maximum. Unloading preserves the history route: the derivative passes through the earlier history. At equality, the implementation can assign half to each input as a convention.

3. What our custom PhAST rule does#

PhAST offers several history choices. Its hard-forward, sigmoid-backward option keeps the maximum in the forward calculation and uses

\[\begin{split} \begin{aligned} w_\psi&=\sigma\!\left(k\frac{\psi^+-H_n}{E_{\rm ref}}\right),\\ w_H&=1-w_\psi,\\ \sigma(s)&=\frac{1}{1+e^{-s}}. \end{aligned} \end{split}\]

in reverse. The operator saves its inputs and applies these weights to the incoming sensitivity. This compact vector–Jacobian product uses the saved inputs directly. The research option uses a fixed reference energy \(E_{\rm ref}\); a dimensional-scale variant also exists. The animation uses dimensionless energies and \(k=12\) to make the transition visible in the teaching example.

Near a switch, this is a surrogate derivative of the hard forward map. A separate smooth-forward option changes the history value itself. Competing crack paths introduce a separate source of branch-dependent sensitivity. Damage bounds and equilibrium solves also need their own consistent backward rules. Our detailed chapter explains those dependencies and tests.

Take away: phase-field regularisation spreads a crack in space. History differentiation carries its memory through time. These solve different parts of the problem.

A pocket chain rule.

Let q = θ2 and L = ½(q − 4)2. At θ = 1, which way does the loss decrease locally?

Follow the derivative

dL/dθ = (q − 4) · 2θ = −6. A sufficiently small positive change in θ decreases the loss. The gradient points uphill; a descent step goes against it.
