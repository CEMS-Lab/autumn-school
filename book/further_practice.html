A differentiable simulation can provide parameter sensitivities on its own. The chain rule was here before the neural network.

A pocket chain rule.

Let q = θ2 and L = ½(q − 4)2. At θ = 1, which way does the loss decrease locally?

Follow the derivative

dL/dθ = (q − 4) · 2θ = −6. A sufficiently small positive change in θ decreases the loss. The gradient points uphill; a descent step goes against it.
