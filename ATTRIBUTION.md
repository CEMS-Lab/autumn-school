# Sources and attribution

The explanatory diagrams, browser interactions and small standalone teaching
models were created for this course. Numerical PhAST figures are generated by
the supplied first notebook. They are not copied from unpublished research runs.

## Software

- [PhAST](https://github.com/CEMS-Lab/PhAST), public revision
  `f6324f899f0701769810be117f27f1208f7a582e`, version 0.16.2.
  The exact source and its MIT licence are included under `vendor/PhAST/`.
- [PyTorch](https://pytorch.org/) provides tensor computation, automatic
  differentiation, model definitions and optimisers.
- [Jupyter](https://jupyter.org/), [Matplotlib](https://matplotlib.org/),
  [Sphinx](https://www.sphinx-doc.org/), [MyST](https://myst-parser.readthedocs.io/)
  and [Beamer](https://ctan.org/pkg/beamer) support the notebook,
  figure, book and presentation formats.

The bundled third-party source retains its licence. A separate licence for
redistribution or adaptation of the original course content has not been
assigned in this package; contact the course authors before republishing it.
This does not alter the rights granted by the bundled software's MIT licence.

## Teaching influences

[Dive into Deep Learning](https://d2l.ai/index.html) informed the integrated
prose, equations, code, output and exercise reading pattern.
[ADL4P](https://tum-pbs.github.io/ADL4P/) informed the short prediction,
reference-comparison and derivative exercises.
[Physics-based Deep Learning](https://physicsbaseddeeplearning.org/intro-teaser.html)
informed the opening question about ambiguous targets; its
[differentiable-physics chapter](https://physicsbaseddeeplearning.org/diffphys.html)
is linked for the numerical-operator viewpoint. Our toy code, figures,
exercises and worked answers were written for this course, not copied from
those books. Their original material is not bundled.

The [CWI Scientific Machine Learning programme](https://github.com/ScientificComputingCWI/SemesterProgramme-SciML)
and [Neural Closure Tutorials](https://github.com/agdestein/NeuralClosureTutorials)
informed the use of a common physical problem, a short equation-to-code route,
prediction questions, and separate evaluation of fitted operators and rollouts.
The downloaded CWI presentations were reviewed as teaching references.
Their PDF pages, figures and unverified Julia scripts are not redistributed.

The course's small data and saved model are generated from its own teaching
equations. They are not particle-inverse or neural-operator paper datasets.
